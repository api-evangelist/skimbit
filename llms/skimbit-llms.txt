# Skimlinks (SkimBit Ltd)

> Skimlinks is a content-monetization and affiliate-marketing platform (part of Taboola) that helps publishers earn commerce revenue from the merchants and products they mention in editorial content. It exposes publisher-facing REST APIs for merchants, reporting and products, an on-page JavaScript monetization script, and a batch Data Pipe for event-level data.

Authentication: all APIs use a timestamp-based `access_token` passed as a query parameter, minted by POSTing your `client_id` and `client_secret` (from the Publisher Hub) with `grant_type=client_credentials` to https://authentication.skimapis.com/access_token. Rate limits are per-endpoint and return HTTP 429 when exceeded.

## APIs

- [Merchant API](https://developers.skimlinks.com/merchant.html): merchant programs, monetizable domains, verticals and offers. Host https://merchants.skimapis.com
- [Reporting API](https://developers.skimlinks.com/reporting.html): commissions, aggregated performance, link/page NDJSON reports, trending products, products bought, payment status, deactivated merchants. Host https://reporting.skimapis.com
- [Product Key API](https://developers.skimlinks.com/product-key.html): product details, pricing, availability and merchant alternatives across 1B+ product offers. Host https://products.skimapis.com
